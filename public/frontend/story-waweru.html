Waweru's journey of resilience

Success story · 17, Kiandutu, Thika

Waweru grew up in Kiandutu, one of Thika's densest informal settlements, after losing both parents by the age of ten. He was taken in by an aunt already supporting her own children on an unstable income, and by his early teens he was working odd jobs instead of attending school.

Finding a way back

AfCiC's outreach team met Waweru through the Working Children Center, which runs daily programmes for children who work rather than attend school full-time. Over eighteen months he moved from occasional attendance to a full vocational training placement in carpentry, funded through the centre.

Passing it on

He now works part-time with a local carpenter while finishing his training, and mentors two younger boys who came through the same outreach programme he did.

“Someone finally asked what I wanted to do, not just what I needed that day.”
— Waweru
