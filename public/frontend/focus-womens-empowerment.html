Women's Empowerment

Focus area
10+ Women's groups actively supported

Challenging norms that hold women back through education, awareness and economic support.

Overview

Many of the families we work with are headed by single mothers balancing casual work with childcare. When a mother's income is unstable, a child's place in school and at home becomes unstable too — so supporting women is child protection by another name.

We run table-banking and savings groups, skills training in trades like tailoring and food processing, and awareness sessions on gender-based violence and women's legal rights, delivered in partnership with local community leaders.

What we do

  • Table-banking and savings groups that build small, steady incomes
  • Vocational skills training in trades women can run from home
  • Awareness sessions on gender-based violence and legal protections
  • Referral support connecting women to health and legal services

Our approach

Groups are community-led once established — our role is to start them, train the first cohort, and stay close enough to help when a member needs more than the group can offer on its own.
