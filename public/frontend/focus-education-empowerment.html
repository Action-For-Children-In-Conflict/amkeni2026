Education Empowerment

Focus area
200+ Children sponsored this term

Sponsoring children from vulnerable backgrounds so school stays a place they return to, not one they leave.

Overview

For a child coming out of poverty or a rescue situation, school is often the first sign that ordinary life is possible again. AfCiC covers fees, uniforms and books for children who would otherwise drop out, and follows their progress year over year.

Sponsorship alone isn't enough if a child is too far behind to keep up, so we pair it with remedial classes and mentorship — adults who check homework, attend school meetings, and notice early when something at home is going wrong.

What we do

  • School fees, uniforms and learning materials for sponsored children
  • Remedial classes for children who have fallen behind their grade level
  • Mentorship pairing each sponsored child with a consistent adult
  • Vocational training pathways for teenagers who won't return to formal school

Our approach

We sponsor individual children rather than schools, so support follows the child through transitions — primary to secondary, or into vocational training — instead of stopping at a single institution's gate.
