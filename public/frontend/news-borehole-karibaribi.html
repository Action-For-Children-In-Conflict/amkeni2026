New borehole brings clean water to Karibaribi centre

Report · 3 July 2026

The Temporary Rescue Center in Karibaribi, Thika, previously relied on trucked-in water during dry spells — an unpredictable cost that competed directly with the centre's food and care budget.

What changed

A newly completed borehole, funded through one of our climate-focused partners, now supplies the centre directly. Early estimates put the water-cost saving at close to a third of what the centre spent during last year's dry season.

Built to last

The project was sized deliberately small: a single borehole the centre's own maintenance team can service, rather than equipment that depends on outside technicians to keep running.

“We used to ration water during the dry months. Now the children just turn on the tap.”
— Centre coordinator, Temporary Rescue Center
