Back-to-school drive reaches 200 children this term

News · 2 September 2026

Every January, April and September, AfCiC runs a back-to-school drive to make sure sponsored children start the term with everything they need — not just fees, but uniforms, shoes and exercise books that families often can't stretch to on their own.

Why the drive matters

This term the drive reached 200 children across our partner schools in Kiambu, Murang'a and Nairobi counties. Case workers visited each family beforehand to confirm what was needed, so support arrived matched to the child rather than handed out as a generic kit.

Our impact

For a number of the children, this was their first full term without a mid-term dropout risk hanging over them — a small logistics win that adds up to years of uninterrupted schooling.

“When my daughter had her own uniform on the first day, she stopped asking if she had to go back to the market instead of school.”
— A parent supported through the programme
