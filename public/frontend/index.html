About us

Action for Children in Conflict (AfCiC) is a Kenyan NGO based in Thika, working across Kiambu, Murang'a and Nairobi counties to protect children and strengthen the families around them.

What drives us

Our mission

To enhance and support the growth of children, young people and their families by facilitating access to justice, education, health, and psychosocial and economic support — with communities at the centre of their own growth.

Where we're headed

Our vision

A world of equal and sustainable opportunities for every child — regardless of where they were born or what they've already been through.
