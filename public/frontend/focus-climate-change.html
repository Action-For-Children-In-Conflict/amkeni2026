Climate Change

Focus area
1 New borehole supplying the Temporary Rescue Center

Promoting renewable energy and sustainable land management so families aren't the first to feel the next drought.

Overview

The families we work with are often the first to feel a failed rainy season — through higher food prices, dried-up wells, and children pulled out of school to help fetch water or work the land. Climate resilience is part of keeping the households around our other programmes stable.

We focus on practical, low-cost interventions: boreholes and rainwater harvesting, kitchen gardens that cut a household's food bill, and tree-planting drives run alongside the schools we already sponsor children through.

What we do

  • Borehole and rainwater harvesting projects at our centres and partner schools
  • Kitchen garden training so households can grow part of their own food
  • Community tree-planting drives in Kiambu and Murang'a
  • Basic climate-smart agriculture training for smallholder families

Our approach

Every project is sized to what a household or school can maintain on its own once we step back — a borehole a community can service, a garden a family can keep watering, not equipment that breaks down after we leave.
